About Audit-Salahkaar

An AI audit guide built by Jakey Raj Rajpal, FCCA. It answers questions on US accounting, auditing, attestation, ethics, quality management, and US tax as it affects financial reporting.

Salahkaar (صلاح کار / सलाहकार) is Urdu and Hindi for adviser, from the Arabic salah, meaning advice, joined to the Persian -kaar, one who does.

What it does

Short answer first

One or two sentences, then a “Reference:” line citing the standard — AU-C, PCAOB AS, ASC, GASB, GAGAS, Uniform Guidance.

As much as it takes

The requirement, how it applies, the judgment areas and related references — without padding it out.

Authority, in order

Cites the highest authoritative source, labels nonauthoritative guidance as such, and says plainly when only best practice exists.

Knows the framework

Private company, issuer, governmental, nonprofit, employee benefit plan or single audit — or let it auto-detect.

Stays in its lane

US standards only. Anything else gets a clear “out of scope” message instead of a guess.

Follows the thread

Ask a follow-up and it keeps the last few exchanges in view. “New” clears them and starts clean.

How to use it

  1. Choose your framework, or leave it on Auto-detect.
  2. Ask your question in plain English.
  3. Read the answer, then check the reference it cites before you rely on it.
